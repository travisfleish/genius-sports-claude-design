## About Genius Sports

Genius Sports is **the Operating System for Modern Sport** — founded in London in 2001 as a betting data specialist, now one of the world's largest sports technology companies. The company connects an entire ecosystem: data and AI foundation (GeniusIQ) → apps and experiences (performance, engagement, betting) → audience signals and advertising inventory. Each layer informs the next.

## Brand Platform

**Vision:** To be everywhere in sports, redefining experiences through technology.

**Mission:** Building the operating system of modern sport — the must-have technology platform for all leagues and teams, fans, sports content distributors, sportsbooks and brands.

**Our Why (purpose):** *Make Sport Even Better.* Technology can't just reflect a trend — it must be purposeful and enhance the game.

**Values (One Genius — "we are one team, being brave, driving change"):**
- **One Team** — collective intelligence toward shared goals, no egos, share what you know.
- **Being Brave** — think big, act boldly, set ambitious goals, revolutionize sport.
- **Driving Change** — growth mindset, seize opportunities, go all-in.

**Brand Principles** (these three govern tone of voice, visual identity, motion, and experience design — always balanced together, never used in isolation):
- **Immediate** — moments matter; real-time, undisturbed experiences that build instant understanding.
- **Intelligent** — AI-first, accurate, insightful, smart, intuitive use of technology and data.
- **Immersive** — driving engagement through compelling narratives that turn passive viewers into active participants.

## Tone of Voice

**Overarching rules:**
- Always speak in the audience's language — explain why it matters to them.
- Don't overplay the sports angle — the brand is as much tech as sport.
- Write in UK English unless the audience is specifically US-based.
- When in doubt, sanity-check copy with a teammate.

**Voice principles in practice:**
| Principle | What it means for copy |
|---|---|
| Immediate | Human, concise sentences. No fluff. Ask "would anyone miss this sentence? If not, delete it." |
| Intelligent | Confident, not boastful. Everyday language over jargon. Include facts that demonstrate expertise without exaggerating. |
| Immersive | Active, punchy verbs (controlled, not casual). Bold, concise opening statements. CTAs start with specific verbs. |

Tone principles flex by product category/audience — e.g. Performance content leans Immediate/Intelligent/Immersive in that order, while Sportsbook content leans Immersive/Intelligent/Immediate. Adjust emphasis, never abandon a principle entirely.

**Example transformation (Website):**
- ❌ "Genius provides sophisticated technological solutions with industry-leading capabilities that facilitate enhanced user experiences across multiple platforms."
- ✅ "Every advantage counts. Our technology helps you watch matches anywhere, bet confidently, and stay protected."

## Visual Identity Philosophy

Colour, typography, imagery, and layout all work together as one cohesive system informed by the brand principles above. See `design-tokens.json` for exact values. Key rules to carry into any generated design:

- **Logo:** Never recreate it — always the supplied artwork, in the correct color per background contrast, with proper clear space.
- **Colour balance:** GS Blue is the hero but must be used *selectively* to protect its impact; GS Navy carries everyday/multi-page material; white must always play a significant compositional role.
- **Graphic Devices:** The Linear Graphic (a stepped-line-weight progression from thin to thick) and the Symbol Supergraphic are the brand's most distinctive, ownable visual assets — more recognizable at a glance than the logo itself. Use real supplied artwork, never redrawn approximations.
- **Typography:** Klarheit Kurrent for display/impact moments (headlines, quotes), Inter for body/UI. Use an em-dash for bullets, echoing the Linear Graphic.
- **Imagery:** Authentic and licensed for real sport/people photography (no AI) — AI generation/enhancement is reserved for scenarios real photography can't capture, and must avoid uncanny or inaccurate details.
- **Iconography:** Google Material Symbols only, colored from the approved palette, used functionally rather than decoratively.
- **Motion:** Every animation should map to Immediate, Intelligent, or Immersive — purposeful, never passive sliding/fading for its own sake.

## Product Groupings

Genius Sports communicates across four distinct product groupings, each with its own visual "flex" of the same core assets:
- **Perform** — modular geometric panels for player stats/interfaces.
- **Bet** (led by BetVision) — UI-inspired lozenge/button shapes and callouts.
- **Engage & Advertising** — layered, bold graphic-device-forward system (builds on the "Built on Genius" campaign language).
- **GeniusIQ** — the ingredient-brand data/AI foundation, with its own logo set, "Powered by GeniusIQ" lockup, and a data-particle narrative visual system (collection → convergence → synthesis → embedding back into the sporting moment).

## Experience & Environmental Design

Physical and digital experiences should "show, don't tell" — activation-led, letting audiences interact with products directly rather than via slides. Materiality is light, bright, textural, and tech-forward (gloss/matte contrast, varnished plywood, polished concrete, backlit LED panels). Digital experiences (especially the website) should be paced and modular — scannable, consistent typography/color/spacing, short sections that keep the same visual language.
